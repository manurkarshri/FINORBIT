# FinOrbit Version 1 user guide

## Start safely

Open FinOrbit in a supported current browser and complete the guided setup. Add at least one active bank or cash account. Enter opening positions as configuration facts; they are not transactions. Consider configuring a passphrase app lock and export an encrypted backup after setup.

## Record daily money

Transactions is the primary workspace. Use a quick action for common expenses, income, transfers, card activity, loan payments, investment activity and refunds, or choose Add transaction for every supported type. Amounts are entered in rupees and stored exactly as integer paise.

Edit creates a replacement with preserved history. Void disables the original effects without deleting the record; eligible voided entries can be restored. Card payments and transfers do not create expenses. Loan payments split principal, interest and fees.

## Plan ahead

Plan contains recurring income and commitments, reminders, budgets, forecasts and goals. Generate recurring occurrences deterministically, then confirm, postpone or skip them. Automatic posting is opt-in and duplicate-protected. Forecasts distinguish confirmed and uncertain items and explain projected shortfalls.

## Understand wealth

Wealth combines active accounts, liabilities, investments, properties, vehicles and other assets. Valuations show source and freshness. Save dated snapshots or recalculate month-end history. Portfolio provider failures preserve holdings and fall back visibly to cached or manual values.

## Reconcile accounts

In Accounts, enter a statement date and closing balance. Review eligible transactions, mark them cleared or reconciled, and complete only at a zero difference. Any balance correction requires an explanation and remains in transaction and audit history. Integrity diagnostics flag possible duplicates, missing references and orphan effects without silently deleting history.

## Create reports

More provides reporting by month, quarter, calendar year, Indian financial year or custom range. Filter Category and Merchant independently or together, and optionally filter Account. Category and Merchant spending appear separately. Export PDF, Excel-compatible XML or CSV; receipts remain excluded except optional counts.

## Protect and recover data

Security, privacy & data provides optional app lock, standard/encrypted backups, restore preview, local reset, storage health and persistent-storage requests. Standard backups omit lock credentials and receipt content. FinOrbit cannot recover forgotten secrets. Read [RECOVERY_GUIDE.md](RECOVERY_GUIDE.md) before resetting data and [PRIVACY_NOTICE.md](PRIVACY_NOTICE.md) for the local-browser privacy model.
